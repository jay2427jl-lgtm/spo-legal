SPO Energy (the "App") is made by Lavnoa LLC, doing business as Seattle Property Operations ("SPO", "we", "us"). Contact: Jason Lyons, Principal, (425) 470-3197.

1. What the App is for

The App helps building owners and their teams track energy benchmarking and building-performance compliance (for example Washington Clean Buildings and Seattle benchmarking/BEPS), enter or import meter data, see deadlines, and prepare reports for a Qualified Person or Qualified Energy Manager. SPO does not certify compliance.

2. Information the App handles

  • Building information you enter: building name, address, size, use type, ESPM property ID, notes.
  • Energy and meter data: monthly usage you type in or import from a CSV or Green Button file.
  • Photos you choose to attach (for example equipment nameplates), picked from your camera or photo library only when you tap to add one.
  • Consent records: which sharing permissions you granted for each building, when, and by whom.
  • Audit log: a record of actions taken in the App (creating or editing a building, importing or deleting readings, granting or revoking consent, approvals, exports).
  • Contact details of people you choose to record (for example a property manager or Qualified Person).

We do not collect payment information. The App does not hold, move, or start payments. The App does not include advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Where your data is stored

Today, everything stays on your device. The App stores your workspace, consent records and audit log in the device's local app storage. SPO cannot see this data unless you export and send it to us.

Optional cloud sync (future). A cloud option (a hosted Postgres database operated by our hosting provider) is designed but not switched on. If we turn it on, we will update this policy first, ask you to opt in, and access will be limited by row-level security so each person sees only buildings they are a member of, in their role.

4. Data from ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager and utilities

If you authorize it (see the Owner Data Consent & Authorization form), SPO may connect to your ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager account through EPA's web services, and may obtain utility data you authorize. We use that data only to provide the services you asked for. You can revoke a connection or share at any time in Portfolio Manager and in the App.

5. How we use information

  • To show readiness, deadlines, and missing data for your buildings.
  • To prepare reports and handoff packets you request.
  • With your consent, to share data with the parties you choose (SPO, your Qualified Person, or, in aggregate form, an agency).
  • Nothing is submitted to any government portal without a recorded human approval by an authorized role (owner or Qualified Person).

6. Sharing

We share building data only: (a) with your consent, for the scope you chose; (b) with service providers who host or process data for us under confidentiality obligations; (c) when required by law. Agencies see only aggregates an owner consented to.

7. Roles

The App recognizes these roles: Building owner, Property manager, Consultant (SPO), Qualified Person, Agency reviewer. Each role has limited permissions (for example, consultants cannot approve submissions; agencies see aggregates only).

8. Retention and deletion

See the Data Retention & Security Statement. You can delete your data and account at any time inside the App: open Settings (or Privacy, terms & consent) and tap Delete my data, then confirm. This permanently erases all App data stored on your device (buildings, equipment, energy data, consents, audit log, requests and settings) and returns the App to its intro screen. Today the App stores no data on our servers; when online accounts launch, the same control will also delete your account and the data we hold on our servers. Uninstalling the App also removes on-device data. You can also request deletion by calling (425) 470-3197.

9. Children

The App is for business users and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Your choices

You may revoke any consent in the App, revoke Portfolio Manager connections/shares, export your data, delete your data in the App with Delete my data, or ask us to delete data we hold by calling (425) 470-3197.

11. Changes

We will post changes in the App and update the effective date.
